About the College

A teaching hospital campus in Bangalore's east

East Point College of Medical Sciences & Research Centre runs its MBBS programme on a single hospital-attached campus, giving students continuous clinical exposure alongside classroom instruction from the first year.

The college is part of the East Point Group of Institutions and functions as a full-fledged teaching hospital, so students train on real patient cases under faculty supervision rather than relying solely on simulated wards.

Apex Learning Network works directly with the admission office on Management Quota and NRI Quota seats, guiding candidates and parents through eligibility checks, documentation, and counselling formalities for the 2026–27 intake.
